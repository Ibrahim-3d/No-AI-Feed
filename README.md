## Install in 60 seconds

1. **[Download the ZIP](https://github.com/Ibrahim-3d/No-AI-Feed/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)**.
2. Extract the ZIP.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
4. Turn on **Developer mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked**.
6. Select the extracted `No-AI-Feed-main` folder.
7. Open Facebook and **refresh once**.

Then click the **No AI Feed** extension icon and choose what you want hidden.

> Already had Facebook open while installing? Refresh the Facebook tab once so the extension can start filtering.

## Your feed, your rules

You decide how aggressive the filter should be.

- **Sensitivity slider** — from obvious AI content to broader AI-related topics.
- **Custom keywords** — add anything you never want to see.
- **Exceptions** — keep specific words or topics visible.
- **Blur mode** — hide a post but keep the option to reveal it.
- **Remove mode** — completely remove matching posts from the feed.
- **Blur strength** — choose how strong the blur should be.

No AI Feed does **not** analyze image pixels or use computer vision.

## FAQ

### How do I hide AI posts on Facebook?
Install No AI Feed, enable the filters you want, and choose **Blur** or **Remove**. The extension can detect Facebook AI labels, supported media metadata and AI-related keywords.

### Can it block ChatGPT, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini or Midjourney posts?
Yes. Keyword filtering includes popular AI tools and topics, and you can add your own words at any time.

### Is No AI Feed an AI image detector?
No. It does not scan image pixels or use AI vision. It filters using platform labels, supported metadata and text rules.

### Does No AI Feed collect my Facebook data?
No. Filtering is designed to happen locally in your browser.
