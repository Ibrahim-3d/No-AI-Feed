# Privacy Policy — No AI Feed

Effective date: September 24, 2026. Applies to version 1.3.2.

No AI Feed is developed by Ibrahim Elrouby. Its single purpose is to filter Facebook and YouTube feed items using settings you choose.

## Data processed locally

The extension reads rendered feed text, AI labels, titles, creator/page/channel names and usernames, relevant links, and media URLs on supported Facebook and YouTube pages. It reads the current supported tab URL to show connection status and offer a current-source shortcut. This is website content and limited browsing activity; it does not obtain your browser history or follow you across unrelated websites.

Your settings, language choice, topic/phrase rules, creator rules, and exceptions are saved in Chrome's local extension storage, not Chrome Sync. Creator rules can contain personal names or usernames. These values remain on your device and are not encrypted by the extension itself. Protect access to your device and browser profile.

Feed information is used only to decide which items to blur or hide, display an explanation, and show current-page filtering status. The extension does not intentionally inspect private messages, passwords, payment information, or authentication cookies.

## Network requests and third parties

No feed content, settings, or browsing activity is sent to the developer. There are no analytics, advertising trackers, remote AI services, or remotely executed code.

When Facebook media metadata detection is enabled, the extension requests media already referenced by the page directly over HTTPS from supported Facebook hosts or Facebook's fbcdn.net media CDN. The destination receives the normal network request, including your IP address and requested media URL. The extension omits credentials and the referrer and refuses redirects. It reads at most the first 2 MiB of the response for local metadata checks, with an eight-second timeout. It does not upload or forward the media to another party. Facebook controls its own processing under its policies.

YouTube filtering uses visible card information. It does not download videos, read transcripts, or analyze image pixels. Facebook metadata detection is best effort and is not proof that an image was or was not generated by AI.

Links to GitHub open only when you select them. GitHub processes your visit and any issue you submit under its own policies. If you voluntarily send a support report, the developer can see its contents and your GitHub identity. Public issues are public: do not include private feed content or sensitive information.

## Retention and deletion

Feed text is not stored persistently by the extension. Metadata results and their media URLs may be cached in service-worker memory, up to 400 entries, and disappear when that worker is terminated or the extension is reloaded. Normal browser HTTP caching may retain fetched media according to browser and server cache settings; this is separate from the extension’s in-memory result cache. Settings and rules persist until edited, deleted, or the extension is uninstalled. Turning filtering off stops new filtering but does not delete settings. Remove an individual rule in Manage filters; remove all local extension data by uninstalling the extension. Previously submitted GitHub support issues are separate from extension storage.

## Limited Use

No AI Feed's use of information received through browser permissions complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only for the stated filtering features, never sold, used for advertising, or transferred for unrelated purposes. The developer cannot remotely access your local feed information or settings.

For ordinary non-sensitive bugs or feature requests, use https://github.com/Ibrahim-3d/No-AI-Feed/issues.
